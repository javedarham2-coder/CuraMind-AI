# Patient Information Schema (CuraMind)

This schema defines all the information collected from the patient before risk assessment.

---

## 1. Personal Information

- Age (Years)
- Gender (Male / Female / Other)
- Date of Birth
- Height (cm)
- Weight (kg)
- BMI (Auto Calculated)

---

## 2. Lifestyle

- Smoking Status
  - Never
  - Former
  - Occasional
  - Regular

- Smoking Frequency (Cigarettes/Day)
- Years of Smoking

- Alcohol Consumption
  - Never
  - Occasional
  - Regular
  - Heavy

- Alcohol Frequency

- Tobacco/Chewing Tobacco
- Physical Activity
  - Sedentary
  - Moderate
  - Active

---

## 3. Family History

- Cancer in Family? (Yes/No)

If Yes:

- Cancer Type
  - Breast
  - Lung
  - Colon
  - Oral
  - Cervical
  - Prostate
  - Other

- Relationship
  - Father
  - Mother
  - Brother
  - Sister
  - Grandparent
  - Other

- Age at Diagnosis
- Multiple Family Members Affected? (Yes/No)

---

## 4. Medical History

- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Heart Disease
- COPD
- Previous Cancer
- Previous Surgeries
- Long-term Medication
- Allergies

---

## 5. Symptoms

- Persistent Cough (>2 weeks)
- Unexplained Weight Loss
- Blood in Stool
- Lump / Swelling
- Fatigue
- Loss of Appetite
- Pain (Specify Area)
- Hoarseness / Voice Change
- Difficulty Swallowing
- Skin Changes
- Non-Healing Ulcer
- Abnormal Bleeding / Discharge
- Other Symptoms

---

## 6. Reproductive Health (If Applicable)

- Menstrual History
- Age at Menarche
- Age at Menopause
- Number of Pregnancies
- Hormone Therapy
- Oral Contraceptive Use

---

## 7. Additional Information

- Occupation
- Exposure to Chemicals
- Radiation Exposure
- Air Pollution Exposure
- Diet Type
- Sleep Duration
- Stress Level
